Don't Say It is the fast, loud party game where you describe a word without saying it - and your team races the clock to guess it.

Create a room, share the 6-letter code, and play from any phone or browser. No sign-up, no ads, no paywalls.

HOW IT WORKS
• One player describes, their team types guesses
• Harder words are worth more points
• Hit a streak and bonus words drop in
• The watching team can call out rule breaks

EVERYTHING IS FREE
• 22 word packs: Easy to Insane, Hindi, Bollywood, Cricket, Movies & TV, Music, Sports, Science & Tech, Travel, Office Life, Festive, Food, Kids & Family
• Custom packs: write your own words - inside jokes, a classroom topic, your team offsite
• 5 game modes: Classic, Blitz (30 seconds!), Marathon, Showdown and Sprint Finish with a double-points finale
• 2 or 3 teams, captain draft or random teams
• 7 colour themes
• Share a results card when the game ends

BUILT FOR GAME NIGHT
• Play together in a room or remotely over a call
• Friends can join from the app or straight from a web link
• Dropped connection? You are put back in your seat automatically
• The host can remove disruptive players, and anyone can report a player

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Games run directly between your devices. No accounts, no tracking, no advertising.
